About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Big Data Science and Industry, Shanxi University, advised by Prof. Yuhua Qian. My research focuses on LLM-driven evolutionary data intelligence and AI scientist agent systems. I also collaborate on research in multimodal learning and visual perception.

我是山西大学大数据科学与产业研究院博士研究生，师从钱宇华教授。主要研究大模型驱动的演化数据智能，聚焦 AI 科学家智能体系统。同时参与多模态学习与视觉感知方向的合作研究。
