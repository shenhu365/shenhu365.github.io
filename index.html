About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Big Data Science and Industry, Shanxi University, advised by Prof. Yuhua Qian. My research focuses on LLM-driven evolutionary data intelligence and AI scientist agent systems.

My recent work connects signal representations with the knowledge of large language models, alongside collaborative research in multimodal learning and visual perception.

我是山西大学大数据科学与产业研究院博士研究生，师从钱宇华教授。主要研究大模型驱动的演化数据智能，聚焦 AI 科学家智能体系统。

近期工作探索信号表征与大语言模型知识的结合，同时参与多模态学习与视觉感知方向的合作研究。
